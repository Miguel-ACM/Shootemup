# Hard patterns: movement and identity

Miguel rejected the previous combat pass as too easy and too similar between bosses. The former Hard balance is now the Easy baseline. Hard replaces the boss scores with evolving attacks shaped around the ship's role; the examples supplied by Miguel are inspiration rather than a uniform feature list.

## Research applied

In [ZUN's Garakuta interview](https://en.touhougarakuta.com/article/specialtaidan_zun_hiroyuki_5-en/), an attack becomes interesting through the identity and context of its owner. In [ZUN and IKD's 2024 discussion](https://news.denfaminicogamer.jp/interview/241225z), geometric beauty is connected to emotion and enjoyable dodging. Our design interpretation is to give each boss a recognizable spatial rule, develop it over time, and make its gaps something the player actively crosses. Naming the old fan differently would not achieve that.

## Implementation and checks

`BossWeave` holds eight fixed scheduling slots, attached to actual weapon transforms. Spirals and ribbons release across multiple frames; web ribs and rings are committed as complete groups. `SimpleRound` gains pooled motion/delivery state for curvature, orbital motion, braking seeds and side trails. Every launch resets that state. The approved circular sprite, exact pixel contact, clearing behavior, wind displacement and shared bloom remain in use. Double orbits use two independent angular terms and an expanding deployment from the visible source; they are moving collidable bullets, not decorative rings.

Before editing, the isolated project recorded former Hard global factors, 76 configured actors and 40 deterministic wave signatures. The promoted Easy configuration matches that snapshot exactly. Hard adds one coordinated answer in a wave's widest suitable rest, tighter small-shot groups and a modest tempo increase. Boss health and damage are unchanged; projectile speed remains restrained.

The visual review covers complete multi-second phrases and both phases, including enough time for delivered rings to reach the player. It records motion families, actual large diameters, pool stability and complete frames after dense releases. The observer review and mathematical checks do not substitute for Miguel's judgment of difficulty.
