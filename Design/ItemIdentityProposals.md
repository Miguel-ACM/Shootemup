# Equipment identities and name proposals

26 October 2026. The original 2023 item pool and the 2025 AK45 keep their names, models and behavior. Another arm and Twerp are original items. Clearer names below now appear for later items; internal type IDs and mechanics remain unchanged.

The model passes replace repeated gun boxes and round sockets with distinct equipment, including: a tuning fork, syringe, five-barrel fan, honeycomb rocket rack, mortar cup, open rail assembly, toothed blade, fuel cylinder, twin capacitor cans, pocket cell, battery rocket, throttle lever, telescope, U-turn, Y-fork, spring, countershot shield, whisker detonator, coin hopper, powder chain, faceted mirror and containment cage. Backdraft and Depth Charge have separate rear-facing hardware. Existing literal everyday props, companions and original recognizable equipment keep their identities; loadout portraits now use their actual original models too.

Some later items have clearer display names for hidden triggers and effects. Their one-line effect remains available when hovering, above the skip zone. No projectile artwork was replaced.
