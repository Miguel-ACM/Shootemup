# Snow fortress: proposed cards

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/SnowSiegeBoss.Proposed.cs`. The fortress keeps its mouth, side ports and the comb on the top edge. Its sides throw only arrows. The comb rains arrows straight down with no stop-and-go. Shell snow is thrown up harmlessly and falls from the top. Pressure is within about 25% of the current cards.

## Phase one (rain)

**Current:** Frozen Petals (mirrored arrow fans stop, glint and re-aim at the pilot) and Icicle Comb (a woven vertical lattice of rain, with loosely aimed mouth rounds).

- **Dendrite Bloom.** Both side ports grow mirrored snow-crystal arms. Each stem is a string of arrows launched at rising speeds, so it lengthens as it flies. Barbs ride beside each link, freeze, then branch off at sixty degrees and accelerate. The fronds sweep from level (covering the upper flanks) to the centre line and back. Slow, accelerating mouth rounds keep the space under them moving. Tones: bright fronds, dark mouth rounds. The hexagonal branching reads as snow, not another re-aimed fan.
- **Sleet Lattice.** The comb rains two crossing families of slanted arrows: even columns lean one way and odd columns the other. The rain falls as a diamond (argyle) lattice. A swinging wind tilts both families together, so the diamond holes drift sideways. Halfway through, the families swap and the holes jump half a diamond. Loosely aimed mouth rounds still fall between beats. Tones: dark rain, bright mouth rounds. Lighter difficulties drop a diagonal of strings rather than whole columns.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Hoarfrost Window*: the comb rains around a rectangular "pane" that slides across the top, and the pane's frame freezes and re-aims.
- *Icicle Drip*: comb columns drip in a slow travelling sine, so the rain falls as a rippling curtain.
- *Rime Feathers*: dendrite barbs fold back parallel to the stem, closing each frond into a three-lane feather.

## Phase two (snow)

**Current:** Whiteout (sideways gusts halt and turn down, with petals and snow shells) and Snowdrift (wide shell fans whose snow falls from the top, with slow stop-and-go orb arcs).

- **Avalanche Cascade.** The mouth lobs shells one after another onto a slanted line: the first lands near and high on one side, the last far and low on the other. They burst in order, so the snow sweeps across the arena from the side the cascade starts on. The next cascade starts from the other side. Arrow streams from the gust ports cross under the bow and open and close like scissors, starting slow and accelerating. Tones: bright shells and snow, dark scissors.
- **Icicle Harp.** The comb is strummed from one side to the other. Each string drops a fast arrow and a slower echo, so every strum falls as two diagonal slashes that draw apart. Two muted strings leave the gap. Strums alternate direction and overlap a little, and the gap walks across the comb. Every other strum, the mouth throws a fan that freezes and re-aims. Now and then a shell's snow falls from the top. Tones: dark strings, bright fans. It follows Miguel's rule that the comb rains in both phases.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Snow Braid*: harmless spray up, then flakes fall in two mirrored braided columns that sway against each other and drift across the width.
- *Cornice*: a row of shells parks along the top edge and bursts from the outside in, closing the snow toward the centre.
- *Frost Heave*: comb strings release in pairs that converge, drawing falling chevrons pointing at the pilot.

## Phase three (Hard)

**Current:** Absolute Zero (the woven comb alternates with fast/slow pairs while petals freeze and re-aim).

- **Diamond Dust.** The comb is strummed from both ends at once, one dark strum and one bright, so each pair falls as a crossing X with a gap in each arm. The side ports grow bright dendrites between strums, after a visible charge. Pressure is similar to Absolute Zero.

Further idea: *Polar Night*: Sleet Lattice and Avalanche Cascade together, with the wind tilting the shells' snow as well.
