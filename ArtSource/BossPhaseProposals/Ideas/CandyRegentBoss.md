# Candy regent: proposed cards

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/CandyRegentBoss.Proposed.cs`. The regent keeps its darting movement in the upper arena and has no minions. Fire leaves its own parts: two prongs at the keel, two mint hatches on the wings and the sugar crown on top. Every card keeps a ricocheting canopy over the top.

## Phase one

**Current:** Taffy Pull (curling slow-to-fast lines at the pilot, with a side ricochet canopy) and Pinball Parlour (ricocheting sprinkles, plus gumdrops that pop into aimed shotguns).

- **Ribbon Candy.** Each hatch pulls two strands of taffy, fast and slow, along one aim that sweeps from 25 degrees below the horizon to 55 above. The walls and ceiling fold the strands into ribbon candy draped over the top, then send them down. Hearts are threaded through the fast strand, and the prongs flick an aimed wrapped sweet now and then. Tones: bright ribbons, dark hearts and sweets. It turns taffy and ricochet into one flowing, mirrored shape.
- **Gumdrop Garden.** The hatches swell and plant a mirrored bed of gumdrops at widening angles and staggered distances. The drops brake, glint, start to sink and pop into small downward cones. The outer drops pop first, rippling inward, and the innermost pair pops at the pilot. Crown stars ricochet off the ceiling between beds. Tones: dark gumdrops (their pops inherit it), bright stars. The pops are planted, timed and pretty, rather than thrown straight at the pilot.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Cotton Candy*: slow swaying clouds of circles that hang over the top and quicken downward one puff at a time.
- *Lollipop Twirl*: hatches spin short mirrored candy-stripe streams that ricochet once.
- *Sprinkle Shower*: stars thrown at the ceiling that come back down in mirrored diagonals.

## Phase two

**Current:** Sugar Rush (a two-arm mirrored lace with taffy pulls), Candy Worms (swaying streams) and Gumdrop Barrage (aimed gumdrop pops).

- **Candy Cane.** Each hatch lays diagonal stripes of arrows and circles, mirrored, alternating bright and dark stripe by stripe like a candy cane. The side walls rebound each stripe back across the other hatch's, forming a crossing lattice. One bead is missing from every stripe, and that opening walks along the stripe. Crown stars cover the ceiling. Tones: alternating stripes, dark stars. The two tones form the actual pattern here, not just an accent.
- **Sweetheart.** After a charge, both prongs release the outline of a heart, leaning apart, that grows as it sinks. Every point of the arena is crossed once by a widening line with room between its beads. Upright hearts alternate with circles. Tones: dark hearts, bright crown stars. It is a new, readable shape that belongs to Candyland, rather than another lace or stream.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Bubblegum*: large rounds swell and drift with sway, then pop into a downward shrapnel cone, while the pilot weaves under them.
- *Wrapped Waltz*: wrapped sweets go out, stop, and turn toward the centre in mirrored pairs, crossing as an X that walks across the arena.
- *Taffy Braid*: two taffy strands with opposite sway, braiding around one aim.

## Phase three (Hard)

**Current:** Sugar Crash (a three-arm lace over candy worms, then gumdrops alternating with worms).

- **Heartbreak.** A large heart grows from between the prongs, stops, and breaks down the middle: each half swings 40 degrees outward. It plays over candy-cane stripes and crown stars. Tones: a bright heart over alternating stripes. Its density is comparable to Sugar Crash's average.

Further idea: *Candy Crush*: gumdrop beds that pop into stripes rather than cones.
