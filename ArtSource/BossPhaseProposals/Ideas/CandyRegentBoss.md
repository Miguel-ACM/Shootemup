# Candy regent: proposed phases

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/CandyRegentBoss.Proposed.cs`. One concept per phase (Miguel, 10 November 2026). Each concept develops through four verses, passing between the regent's own parts: the prongs at the keel, the mint hatches on the wings and the sugar crown on top. The regent keeps darting through the upper arena with no minions. Every shape is a line with open lanes, never a scatter, and HeartRound is only an accent (the earlier recording read as a carpet of hearts).

## Phase one: Ribbon Candy

**Current:** Taffy Pull and Pinball Parlour alternate (about 30 rounds per second on average).

Mirrored taffy ribbons: two strands, fast and slow, along one sweeping aim. They are dashed five beats on and two off, so every fold has gaps. The walls and ceiling fold them into ribbon candy. The fold period tightens every pass.
1. **Hatch ribbons.** The hatches sweep from below the horizon to above it, folding over the top.
2. **Crown ribbons.** The crown throws ribbons up that drape off the ceiling; this verse is the canopy.
3. **Prong ribbons.** The prongs fold ribbons down and out off the side walls into the lower arena.
4. **Braid.** Hatches and prongs alternate beats in antiphase.

Tones: bright ribbons with a dark heart every sixth bead. Density: about 29 rounds per second.

Alternative single concepts:
- *Gumdrop Garden*: mirrored beds of gumdrops that brake, glint and pop into downward cones in ripples. Verses change the ripple order and which parts plant.
- *Taffy Braid*: two swaying strands braid around one aim, and the braid tightens.
- *Cotton Candy*: swaying clouds of circles hang at the top and quicken downward one puff at a time.

## Phase two: Candy Cane

**Current:** Sugar Rush, Candy Worms and Gumdrop Barrage (about 39 rounds per second on average).

Mirrored diagonal stripes of arrows and circles alternate bright and dark like a candy cane. Each stripe has one bead missing, and that opening walks along the stripe, back the other way each pass. The walls rebound the stripes across each other. Every fourth stripe comes from the crown and goes up off the ceiling.
1. **Hatch stripes** run down and out.
2. **Prong stripes** cross beneath the regent.
3. **Crown stripes only**: the whole canopy.
4. **Hatches and crown** alternate.

Density: about 39 rounds per second.

Alternative single concepts:
- *Sweetheart*: mirrored heart outlines that grow as they sink (needs sparser hearts than the first recording).
- *Wrapped Waltz*: wrapped sweets go out, stop, and turn toward the centre in mirrored pairs, forming an X that walks across the arena.
- *Pinball Lanes*: sprinkles ricochet into fixed diagonal lanes that shift each verse.

## Phase three (Hard): Heartbreak

**Current:** Sugar Crash (about 62 rounds per second on average).

Heart outlines (arrows, with an upright heart every fourth bead) grow from the regent and sink. They then stop and break down the middle: the halves swing apart and leave a lane that follows the regent's dashes.
1. A large heart with a smaller one nested inside.
2. Twin hearts from the hatches.
3. The large heart between two hatch hearts.
4. A ripple of three hearts in quick succession, each larger than the last.

Tones: bright outer hearts, dark inner and hatch hearts. Density: about 60 rounds per second.

Alternative single concepts:
- *Sugar Crash*: candy-cane stripes that stop mid-air and fall apart into taffy pulls.
- *Candy Crush*: gumdrop beds that pop into short stripes instead of cones.
- *Lollipop*: mirrored dashed ribbons swinging in a slowing pendulum that hangs and releases.
