# Black hole: proposed cards

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/SpaceCitadelBoss.Proposed.cs`. Fire leaves the singularity or the two eyes in its event horizon. Inward fronts are the one exception, as in the shipping book: they are born on one circle beyond the whole screen and close on the core, with no nested inner circles and no orbit tells. Circles always move. The black hole keeps its upper station.

## Phase one

**Current:** Accretion (uniform circular fronts closing from beyond the screen, with dark-star fronts and comets) and Supernova (rings of arrows that stop and collapse back through the core, then the laser).

- **Accretion Weave.** Every circular front is woven from two strands: arrows tilted one way and circles tilted the other. As a front closes, the strands swirl against each other, so successive fronts cross into a shrinking lattice that the core swallows. A slowly turning seam stays open. The eyes throw dark stars up over the top of the arena; gravity bends them round and the core swallows them. Tones: bright arrows, dark circles and stars. The fronts are still born on the single circle beyond the screen, but they read as a galaxy lattice rather than plain rings.
- **Eclipse.** After a charge at the eyes, each eye throws a ring whose centre drifts out to its own side while it opens, so the two rings stop overlapping like an eclipse. They hold, retrace their paths through the black hole and open inverted on the other side, crossing each other. Between eclipses, a comet with a bead tail leaves one eye at the pilot. Tones: dark rings with bright crescents, bright comets. This is still a supernova that collapses back through the core, but with two moving, crossing circles.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Lensing*: a front that bends visibly around the core's well before it is swallowed (Attract instead of Absorb).
- *Event Horizon Blink*: the eyes close, the disk dims, and a front arrives on the next blink.
- *Tidal Tails*: inward fronts with alternating fast and slow beads that separate into two rings while closing.

## Phase two

**Current:** Expulsion (mirrored spiral arms flung out, with rings and dark stars) and Binary Collapse (two supernova rings at once, then the laser).

- **Orrery.** The core flings planets: small rings of circles that bloom, spin as they travel out on six mirrored headings (left planets turning one way, right planets the other), and sling their moons off on the tangent together. Comets with bead tails leave the eyes to deny a still pilot. Tones: bright planets, dark comets. These are moving circles, as Miguel asked for Space, in place of another spiral.
- **Pulsar.** Star-shaped shells (five points tip down, then six points tip up) open, hold, then collapse through the core in proportion to their reach, so each opens again as the same star turned over. Mirrored pulsing beams of arrows and crescents sweep round from the eyes in opposite directions. The centre laser ends the card. Tones: dark shells with bright beads, bright beams.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Binary Star*: two planets orbit each other while travelling out, then sling apart.
- *Gravity Assist*: planets pass close to the core and are flung onto new headings.
- *Pulsar Glitch*: the beams stop, reverse and change pulse length on a visible flicker of the eyes.

## Phase three (Hard)

**Current:** Big Crunch (fronts closing from beyond the screen, trading dominance with outward spiral arms and dark stars).

- **Galaxy Collision.** Woven fronts close in from the circle beyond the screen while the core keeps flinging planets out through them and the eyes throw dark stars. Tones: bright fronts, dark planets. It is about 20% lighter than Big Crunch's peak, but the crossing motion makes it harder to read through.
  - Concern: the outward planets are rings born at the core. They are not the inner front circles or orbit tells Miguel rejected, but he should check this against that note.

Further idea: *Heat Death*: fronts that slow as they close, while planets speed up, so the two motions trade dominance.
