# Black hole: proposed phases

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/SpaceCitadelBoss.Proposed.cs`. One concept per phase (Miguel, 10 November 2026). Each concept develops through four verses, then repeats them mirrored. Fire leaves the singularity or the two eyes in its event horizon. Inward fronts are the one exception, as in the shipping book: they are born on one circle beyond the whole screen and close on the core, with no nested inner circles and no orbit tells. Circles always move.

## Phase one: Accretion Weave

**Current:** Accretion and Supernova alternate (about 45 rounds per second on average).

Every circular front is woven from two strands: arrows tilted one way and circles tilted the other. Successive fronts therefore cross into a shrinking lattice that the core swallows, and a slowly turning seam stays open.
1. **Weave.**
2. **Tightening weave**: the tilt grows through the verse.
3. **Tidal weave**: arrows outrun circles, so each front splits into two crossing rings.
4. **Fountain**: the eyes throw dark stars up over the top, and gravity bends them back into the core.

On the second pass the strands and the seam turn the other way.

Tones: bright arrows, dark circles and dark stars. Density: about 44 rounds per second.

Alternative single concepts:
- *Eclipse*: rings from both eyes drift apart, hold, and fall back through the core inverted. Verses change ring size, drift and shape.
- *Lensing*: fronts bend visibly around the well before they are swallowed (Attract instead of Absorb).
- *Event Horizon Blink*: fronts arrive on the eyes' blinks, and the blink rhythm quickens.

## Phase two: Orrery

**Current:** Expulsion and Binary Collapse alternate (about 59 rounds per second on average).

The core flings planets: small spinning rings of circles that travel out on six mirrored headings, with left planets turning one way and right planets the other. The planets then sling their moons off on the tangent together. Comets with bead tails leave the eyes to deny a still pilot. Each pass reverses the spins.
1. **Planets.**
2. **Epicycles**: every moon carries a satellite, drawing spirograph loops.
3. **Swelling worlds**: the planets grow as they travel and sling late.
4. **Slingshot galaxy**: planets on the in-between headings sling early, close to the core.

Tones: bright planets, dark comets. Density: about 61 rounds per second.

Alternative single concepts:
- *Pulsar*: star-shaped shells collapse through the core inverted under sweeping mirrored eye beams, closing with the laser.
- *Binary Star*: pairs of planets orbit each other while travelling, then sling apart.
- *Gravity Assist*: planets pass the core and are flung onto new headings.

## Phase three (Hard): Crunch and Rebound

**Current:** Big Crunch (about 165 rounds per second).

Woven fronts close from the circle beyond the screen. Every front the core swallows comes back out on the beat it arrives, after a brief cue at the core.
1. **Bounce**: a ring of arrows thrown out.
2. **Spin**: a spinning ring of circles that grows outward.
3. **Echo**: a ring that stops and falls back through the core.
4. **Swirl**: a woven ring of arrows and crescents leaving with both tilts.

Tones: bright fronts, dark rebounds. Density: about 155 rounds per second.

Alternative single concepts:
- *Galaxy Collision*: woven fronts in, Orrery planets out.
- *Heat Death*: fronts slow as they close while the rebounds speed up, trading dominance.
- *Spaghettification*: each front stretches into a spiral as it nears the core.
