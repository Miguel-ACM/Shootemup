# Seraph Judicator (Heaven): proposed phases

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/SeraphJudicatorBoss.Proposed.cs`. The only shipping edit is the `partial` keyword on the class.

Each phase is one concept that runs for the whole phase. It develops in movements of about 5.5 s. Each movement opens with a prayer and a charge at the core eye and ends with a short rest, plus a longer breath after every fourth. The movement number picks one of four variations, and each completed cycle of four escalates. Over a 60 s recording that is about ten movements, so every variation plays twice and the second cycle is visibly faster.

`CardIndex` alternates by movement, which also flips the dominant tone. The review tools read it as card A/B.

The angel's anatomy draws everything:

- **Wheels.** Lashes leave the eyes of the tumbling wheels along their own projected outline, around one moving aisle.
- **Lances.** Lances leave the core eye or the outer side eyes. Side lances ride their eye as the shipping lances do. Thin aimed lances stay pinned where the eye opened, because a riding beam would slide onto a pilot who had already stepped clear.
- **Empty air.** Unbodied eyes (the six choir glows) summon fire at authored sites.

Rounds are feathers, stars, pearls and scored needles. Nothing bounces along the ceiling.

Rough average pressure on Hard, in rounds per second over a whole loop including rests:

| Phase | Proposed | Shipping phase | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One | ~170 | ~190 | -12% |
| Two | ~230 | ~260 | -11% |
| Three | ~320 | ~345 | -7% |

## Phase one: Wheel Within a Wheel

**Current:** Dawn Liturgy and Cathedral of Eyes alternate (lance-framed feather vaults and windows), each with the unbodied choir.

**Wheel Within a Wheel** (Ezekiel 1:16): the eyes of the tumbling wheels are the weapon. Every lash is drawn along a wheel's own outline around one swaying aisle, and a different wheel takes it up each movement. Mid-movement, the core eye lays a wide lance along the pilot's line.

| Movement | Development |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The middle wheel lashes; the outer side eyes answer with mirrored rim fans, each with a drifting open lane. |
| 2 | The outer wheel takes it up, wider and slower, over the inner wheel's weeping pearls. |
| 3 | The middle and inner wheels trade beats, feathers and stars against pearls, with mirrored aisles. |
| 4 | Every eye opens: the upper rims hang feathers along the top edge that fall back as rain, and watchers in the corners fan across the far floor. |

Each later cycle sways the aisle 25% faster and widens every lash by one round.

Other single concepts:
- *Eyelid Blink*: all the wheels' eyes fire together, and a wave of closed eyes travels round each rim. The blink is the moving gap.
- *Ladder of Jacob*: two rail lances from the side eyes, with star rungs that climb and descend between them, faster each movement.

## Phase two: Six Wings

**Current:** Sevenfold Procession and Judgment Pendulum alternate (a wide lace between lances; a sweeping lance over pearled ribs), with the choir. The recording read as a near-carpet of feathers.

**Six Wings** (Isaiah 6:2): the seraph opens one pair of wings per movement and all six in the fourth. Every attack is a wing shape: drawn, held, then shed, so the structure carries the pressure.

| Movement | Development |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (flight) | Two layered wings are thrown sideways and halt as drawn wings, then shed straight down as curtains beside the walls. The centre lane stays clear, and a rest beat in every four opens passages through the curtains. Holy eyes along the top drop chords that halt, stare and are thrown at the pilot. |
| 2 (feet) | A two-layer skirt of arrows and beads beneath the body opens outward and folds back with the wing beat. The side eyes stare at a still pilot. |
| 3 (face) | Two lances from the riding outer eyes are held out to the walls and beat down and back. Raised wings hang along the top edge and shed down the upper sides over a light skirt. |
| 4 (Holy, holy, holy) | All six together, each pair lighter, under the Holy chorus. |

Each later cycle beats the wings 20% faster: a faster stroke, and the wing lances sweep faster.

Other single concepts:
- *Feather Fall*: plumes along the whole top edge shed swaying feathers, while gate lances cross beneath the angel. It was rejected as the phase concept because it read as a carpet.
- *Censer*: a pearl plume hung from the lowest eye swings wider each movement and scatters stars at the end of each swing.
- *Many Eyes*: rim eyes open in turn, laying thin crossing lances with feather lids. In this version it lives inside phase three.

## Phase three (Hard): Sea of Glass

**Current:** Last Trumpet and Covenant alternate (a central column with lancets; closing scythes), over the densest feather banks.

**Sea of Glass** (Revelation 4:6 and 15:2): unbodied eyes draw panes of pearls that hang and then move as one. Sheets fall as a woven lattice, columns slide sideways, and every pane keeps one open seam. Beneath, the outer wheel's lashes stand for waves around a moving aisle.

| Movement | Development |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (still sea) | Two sheets per verse fall on alternating diagonals. Their woven channels shift half a step each pane, so no channel is a resting place. |
| 2 (many eyes) | The same sheets, while pairs of outer rim eyes lay thin aimed lances through them that cross on the pilot, with feather lids along each line. |
| 3 (the sea stands up) | Two columns stand left and right of the middle and slide outward or inward as walls, each with a climbing or sinking seam. |
| 4 (mingled with fire) | One sheet and one column per verse, with the many eyes' lances. |

Each later cycle moves the wave aisle faster.

Other single concepts:
- *Wheels of Fire*: two wheels lash at a time, interleaving the three outlines around one aisle, under a slow propeller lance through the core eye.
- *Throne of Sapphire*: four pinned lances charge a square around the pilot's position, feathers fall inside it, and the square shrinks each movement.
- *Last Seal*: a counted seven-beat procession in which each wheel fires alone in turn. Each movement adds a seal and ends in a full-width pane.

## Concerns

- The lashes depend on the wheels' 3D tumble. When a wheel turns edge-on, its lower rim holds fewer eyes and that beat is lighter.
- In Six Wings movement 3, a rising outer eye lifts its wall lance toward the ceiling. This shrinks, but does not close, the corner pocket above it, and the 1.6 s charge shows the line first.
- Phase three keeps the wheel lash as its largest share of rounds, so that the pressure stays within the target. It has a single aisle and reads as spokes, not a carpet, but should be checked in the recording.
