# Hell crab: proposed cards

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/HellCrabBoss.Proposed.cs`. No shipping file changed.

Every card is timed to the claw stroke the crab already flies:
- raise
- come down to the pilot's height
- sweep inward
- return
- rest

The pincers fire on that stroke. The eyes stare and the mouth throws the brimstone. There are no minions and no vent fire. Rounds: Hell flames, souls and beads (circles), arrows. Hostile fire stays in the mint district hue. Each card's dominant tone alternates with the previous card's.

Pressure on Hard: about 17-20 rounds/s in phase one, against about 17 for the current cards, and about 40-47 in phase two, against about 50.

## Phase one

**Current:** Pincer Scissors (aimed streams from both pincers crossing over the pilot, with mouth chevrons) and Soul Procession (eye souls that stop and dart, under a bouncing canopy).

- **Ember Cage** (dark dominant). As the pincers come down to the pilot's height, the pincers and the eyes throw dark flames that all stop at once as a cage around the pilot. The pincers lay a bar on each side and the eyes lay the lid. A bright second frame stops just outside. The cage stares, then closes: the bars slide together and cross while the lid comes down, so the open floor is the way out. The outer frame closes a beat later. The real claws sweep in at the same moment, so the claws clamp the cage. Four bright eye glares (graded arrow lines straight at the pilot) answer while the claws rest. It is a trap built from the crab's own parts, where the current card has only streams.
- **Lantern Garland** (bright dominant). The eyes string a garland of bright souls and dark flames from wall to wall across the upper arena. The first garland sags under the crab, with its ends in the top corners. The second arches over the crab, with its ends low at the sides. The souls stare, then dart at the pilot from the middle outward, while the flames unzip off the ends. Through the claw sweep, each pincer snips four times with two bright arrow blades either side of its aim. The four blades cross into scissors over the pilot. The garland closes the corners and the ceiling, so the top is never safe.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Molting*: flames shed from the carapace outline that hang in the crab's silhouette, then fall away as a shell.
- *Tidepool Souls*: souls that orbit one claw tip while it lowers and are flung off tangentially at the sweep.
- *Ember Lattice*: two cages offset diagonally that close in alternation.

## Phase two

**Current:** Pincer Inferno (faster scissors alternating with the canopy) and Inferno Lace (a three-arm lace from the moving pincers while bright souls stare).

- **Brimstone Braid** (dark dominant). Each moving pincer pours two dark flame strands that sway in opposite phase toward the far lower corner. They plait into a braid, and the two braids cross under the crab. A bright knot runs down each braid's axis. Every eleven beats the braid opens for three, so a gap travels down it. As the claws lower and sweep, the braids bend across the arena. The mouth hangs a bright brimstone lattice in a row along the ceiling. Its arrows fall away in alternating diagonals that cross on the way down, offset by half a lane each time. This replaces the ceiling bounce with a lattice that hangs first, then falls.
- **Searing Trace** (bright dominant). Through their stroke, the pincers leave a bright trail of flames that stop almost where the pincer passed. Each flame stares, then darts at the pilot 1.1 s after it was laid. The fuse burns along the claws' path, and the trail draws the claw motion in fire. While the claws rest, the mouth answers with three dark falling arrow wavefronts in three speed layers, tilted left, centre and right.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Hellgate*: the pincers meet at the sweep and open a vertical gap of flames between them that the pilot must pass through as it closes.
- *Penance Chains*: two rows of souls tethered between the eyes and claws that swing like a pendulum before snapping toward the pilot.
- *Cinder Wheel*: a spoke pattern of short flame lines from the mouth that turns as a unit (not a ring: the spokes have no rim).

## Inferno (Hard, phase three)

**Current:** a three-arm bright lace from the moving pincers with dark scissors and souls.

- **Ninth Circle.** In every claw stroke the pincers draw a dark searing trace through the whole stroke. At the pilot's height, the eyes and pincers drop a bright ember cage, so the trace darts through a closing cage. While the claws rest, two bright brimstone lattices fall from the ceiling. It combines the phase-one cage with the phase-two trace and ceiling lattice.

Further ideas (not implemented):
- *Judecca*: the cage freezes (holds twice as long) while the trace keeps darting, then everything releases on one beat.
