# Hell crab: proposed cards

Code: `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/Bosses/HellCrabBoss.Proposed.cs`. No shipping file changed.

**One concept per phase**, developed stroke by stroke on the claw stroke the crab already flies:
- raise
- come down to the pilot's height
- sweep inward
- return
- rest

Each concept rotates through three variations of itself, so a 60-second recording shows every variation at least twice. Fire leaves only the pincers (the tip and both jaws), the eyes and the mouth. There are no minions and no vent fire. Rounds: Hell flames, arrows and beads, in the mint district hue. Flames stay within the warmed reserve because the ribbons put a flame at the tip and arrows at the jaws.

## Phase one: Ember Cage

**Current:** Pincer Scissors with mouth chevrons, alternating with Soul Procession under a bouncing canopy.

Every stroke, as the pincers come down, the pincers and eyes throw dark flames that all stop at once as a cage around the pilot. The pincers lay a bar on each side and the eyes lay the lid, with a bright second frame just outside. The real claws sweep in at the same moment and clamp it. While the claws rest, the jailer's eyes glare: graded arrow lines straight at the pilot, three, four, then five. The stroke rotates through three ways the cage leaves:
1. *Closing*: the bars slide together and cross while the lid comes down; the outer frame follows a beat later. The floor is the way out.
2. *Staring*: every flame stares, then darts at the pilot: the left bar, then the right, then the lid.
3. *Bursting*: the inner cage flies apart outward while the bright outer frame closes through it.

Alternative single concepts (not implemented):
- *Lantern Garland*: a wall-to-wall garland of souls and flames that sags or arches each stroke; souls dart from the middle out, flames unzip. It was implemented in an earlier draft.
- *Pincer Snips*: crossing arrow blades from both pincers through every sweep, opening wider and snipping more often stroke by stroke.
- *Molting*: flames shed from the carapace outline hang in the crab's silhouette and fall away as a shell, a different plate each stroke.

## Phase two: Searing Trace

**Current:** Pincer Inferno (faster scissors alternating with the canopy), then Inferno Lace (a pincer lace while souls stare).

Through each stroke the pincers draw their path in a bright ribbon: a flame from the tip between arrows from the jaws, hanging almost where they passed. The stroke rotates through three ways the drawing burns:
1. *Fuse*: each ember darts at the pilot 1.1 s after it was laid, so the fire runs along the claws' path.
2. *Brand*: the whole drawn shape hangs until the claws rest, then darts at the pilot in drawing order.
3. *Rift*: at the rest the ribbon tears open; the jaw arrows drift apart on either side of the path and the tip flames sink down it.

The rest after the stroke belongs to the fire: the fuse or the brand burns out before the next stroke.

Alternative single concepts (not implemented):
- *Brimstone Braid*: two counter-swaying flame strands per moving pincer plait into braids that cross under the crab. It was implemented in an earlier draft.
- *Hellgate*: the pincers meet at the sweep and open a vertical gate of flames that closes while the pilot passes, narrower each stroke.
- *Penance Chains*: rows of souls tethered between the eyes and claws swing like a pendulum before snapping toward the pilot.

## Inferno (Hard, phase three): Ninth Circle

**Current:** a three-arm bright lace from the moving pincers with dark scissors and souls.

The crab digs a pit in fire on every stroke:
- As the pincers descend at the sides, they draw the pit's walls as dark ribbons.
- The eyes seal its ceiling with a three-row lid along the top edge that falls slanting, with one gap that moves from lid to lid.
- The walls close as the claws sweep in. They slide in, stare and dart, or slide in slowly, rotating stroke by stroke.
- The sweep's own ribbon burns like a fuse at the pilot.
- While the claws rest, two more lids fall from the ceiling.

Alternative single concepts (not implemented):
- *Judecca*: the cage freezes, holding twice as long while the trace keeps darting, then everything releases on one beat.
- *Ember Cage Unbound*: the phase-one cage built twice per stroke, at the pilot and where the pilot fled.
- *Cocytus Spiral*: claw ribbons that rotate around the pilot's last position as the claws sweep (arcs, not circles).
