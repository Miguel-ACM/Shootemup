Rapid giant side-spray - 6 November 2026

on every shot. The heading follows the carrier's current direction, with +/-9 degrees
of skew and +/-10% speed variation. Hard intervals are 0.04-0.07 seconds; Normal and
Easy scale that cadence through the existing density and rest settings. Preserve the
initial warning, brief release pulses, small giant hitbox and approved projectile art.

difficulties. All six encounters fired the spray and reused warmed projectile objects.
