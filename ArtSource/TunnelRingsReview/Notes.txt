Tunnel phase-one ring correction, 6 November 2026

Frogger Crossing previously kept every ring at 48 degrees per second regardless
of radius. At radius 14.5, that produces about 12.15 world units per second of
tangential travel, in addition to the outward expansion.

The initial angular rate is now 30 degrees per second. The optional linear
expansion cap smoothly reduces angular speed once tangential travel reaches
2.8 units per second. Its angle uses the exact integral of speed/radius, making
the trajectory independent of frame rate and preserving the slingshot tangent.
The outward expansion stays at 1.3 units per second. The initial launch from the
boss centre, alternating directions, six rings, stations and later phases stay.

Density increases from 30 to 36 authored positions, with four missing positions
instead of three to preserve readable crossings. Actual teeth per ring:
Easy 10 -> 11; Normal 17 -> 19; Hard 27 -> 32.

The cap is opt-in. Existing Space and other orbit programs retain their original
trajectories, and pooled reuse resets the cap. Pixel sprites and collision
geometry are unchanged. The collision activation speed remains conservative.

One isolated command-line gameplay batch checked phase one on all three tiers,
1200 complete frames each at 60 Hz, with native physics and rendering excluded.
All three reached radii over 14 while remaining below 2.8 tangential units/s.
No projectile objects were constructed during the checked phases. Hard peaked
at 336 rounds; complete CPU frame p95 was 2.870 ms, maximum 6.117 ms.
Both rotation directions stayed below 3.10 combined radial/tangential units/s
after the initial launch. Pooled reuse and unchanged default orbit paths passed.
