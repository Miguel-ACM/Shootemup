Giant side-spray rounds, 6 November 2026

The approved GiantOrb gains an opt-in SideSpray program. It survives each burst
and sends small circular rounds out from both rims, perpendicular to its travel.
The release delay, flank angle and shot speed vary locally. A 0.24-second pulse
precedes each burst; the source artwork, collision shape and district tint stay
unchanged. Easy/Normal/Hard release 1/2/3 rounds per side with the existing Rest
scaling. Children inherit damage. Offscreen, held, fading and cleared orbs do not
continue side fire. The state resets when an ordinary orb reuses the same object.

SnowSiegeCraft now rotates snow rain, freezing fans and paired giant snowballs.
Its snow still begins above the visible screen. TaffyDuelist replaces its popping
gumdrop move with two side-spraying giant gumdrops; both launch ports now warn.
Wave Watcher includes GIANT ORB / SIDE SPRAY under Projectiles.

One isolated command-line review covered both actual minibosses on all three
difficulties, pooled state reset, irregular mirrored side releases, inherited
damage and pixel contacts, offscreen suppression, clearing and the live cap.
All checks passed without creating projectile objects during the encounters.
