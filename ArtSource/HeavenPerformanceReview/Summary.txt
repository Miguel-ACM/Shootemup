Heaven performance and final-boss corrections — 5 November 2026

Measured cause
The angel wrote 36 moving eye paths into one 37-path PolygonCollider2D each frame.
Each SetPath rebuilt the complete collider. Separate one-path colliders preserve
the core and all 36 moving eye outlines, their native contacts and damage owner.
The projection also reuses the eye outline and one matrix per eye.

Matched Editor CPU profile, original Heaven Hard phase-three score (1800 frames):
                             Before       Collider fix
Mean eye collider update     6.628 ms      0.329 ms
Mean complete CPU frame      18.934 ms     8.569 ms
95th-percentile CPU frame    28.752 ms     12.975 ms
Peak hostile rounds          2259          2257
New bullet objects           0             0

A second issue allocated a params array in ContactSpeed for every round every
frame. The equivalent nested two-argument Max calls allocate nothing: 10,000
contact-speed evaluations were measured at zero managed bytes.

Standalone development-player CPU profile, revised attacks, native physics
included; rendering excluded:
- Heaven Hard phase three: 2336 peak rounds, CPU p50 4.512 ms, p95 7.430 ms,
  maximum 12.549 ms across 1800 frames.
- Sustained 3600-round bouncing stress: CPU p50 10.217 ms, p95 14.879 ms,
  maximum 21.052 ms across 600 frames.
- No new bullet objects in either measured workload. Live/pool budgets remain
  3600/7400. These are CPU results, not GPU measurements or a guarantee that
  every possible loadout and scene will stay within a frame budget.

Preserved behavior
- All 37 eye/core damage shapes were compared through 120 tumbling poses;
  maximum vertex error was below 0.000001 world units.
- Beam, drill, active, area, burning-trail and companion queries include all
  root damage colliders. Each beam still damages once per target per tick.
- Legacy single-hit projectiles resolve only one contact even when several
  overlapping eyes report a hit before their deferred destruction.
- Phase gates, bullet art, colors, exact projectile contacts and boss health
  are unchanged.

Requested changes
- Void proceeds from its finale to victory without a boss item decision.
  Remaining coins are collected exactly once before the run is banked.
- Heaven and Void devote roughly 73% of their paired-bank rounds to the
  lower arena. Heaven's lower fans are less horizontal. Void's final star
  sends three arms downward and keeps a smaller upper answer.
- The final star's two side tips charge for 0.45 seconds, then fire across
  the pilot's recorded position. Four stationary positions 0.9–2.5 units
  above its centre were reached in the native simulation.
- Ordinary and miniboss laser holds are about 40–45% shorter. Warning time
  is preserved; short Heaven beams complete their widening sooner. District
  boss beam holds are preserved.

Validation notes

bounced off the bottom. They intentionally do not. The corrected workload
refills returned pool slots to maintain 3600 rounds; that targeted rerun passed.
GameplayChecks-Initial.txt retains the original failed assertion for context.
Checks.txt combines the passed gameplay and corrected stress checks.

it is not part of the production game source.
