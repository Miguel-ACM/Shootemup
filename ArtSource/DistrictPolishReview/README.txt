DISTRICT POLISH / PACING / DIFFICULTY REVIEW

Every district opens with a free item decision and has seven ordinary combat waves, two pre-miniboss encounter selections and two further selections earned by killing its dedicated miniboss within 20 seconds. The timer starts when the miniboss enters the arena. Failure makes it stop firing and visibly retreat; it grants neither kill credit nor the two bonus decisions. The countdown and health meter use the actual side HUD. Ordinary waves still overlap a little; decisions and major fights wait for clear combat.

All routes and encounter holds use two seconds. Shops, free rewards and boss items can be skipped in a full-width bottom zone 145/720 of the screen high. Shops retain multiple purchases. Opaque offer plates are smaller and fade in after settling; passive/active/weapon text sits beneath the model with a small outline. The latest layout reference is Shop-FinalLabels.png.

Hell now has broad orange lava rivers/pools, crater-attached fire and real warned volcano eruptions. Random red scenery glints were removed. The crab uses layered carapace plates and sixteen overlapping armored arm sections. Underwater has stronger soft light shafts, caustics, branching colored coral and swimming fish schools/a ray with seabed shadows. The submarine was remodeled with a stepped sail, rounded bow, pressure-hull plating, torpedo banks, hydroplanes, vents and periscopes. Its full hull has a capsule hitbox, and it attacks with three offset crowns plus dense six-lane charge wakes.

Ten distinct timed miniboss fights replace the former ordinary-enemy guards. Five existing heavy craft now have authored district-specific movement; new champions are the scarab dredger, taffy duelist, orbital warden, reef leviathan and infernal ferryman. All are exposed through the enemy editor and Wave Watcher.

Firing cues use one small reused muzzle sprite per ship: visible charge and a brief release, without per-bullet lights or replacement projectile art. Newly visible standalone ships get a short first-shot windup. Hell imps fire three S rounds on two accents rather than five on four; helix speed, amplitude and curvature were reduced. Only Plains' horizontal-release carrier is orange, while its child rounds keep the normal palette. Plains boss base health is 1200 and scored firing density is approximately halved. Thunder base health is 1250; its six attached lasers smoothly reverse at 60-degree endpoints and its outward gun phrases now angle downward.

Difficulty comparison
Balance.txt is the initial uniform first-five-seconds snapshot across all ten districts, with the observer not killing enemies. Counts alone do not express threat: City/Thunder/Space have beams and quick aimed shots; Tunnel retains ricochets, Snow shells fragment, and Underwater deliberately has lower wave density plus slower handling. The review strengthened Space through three/four layered motifs, guaranteed emitter/carrier phrases, stronger satellites and independent citadel pressure. Snow bombers now have fewer shells and the augers alternate mortars; the corrected sampled Snow peak is 51 instead of 102. Tunnel wall batteries fire three spaced ricochets rather than one. Hell retains the highest tier and strongest guard health budget. Boss health does not scale with the player's items; a real runtime loadout-change check confirmed unchanged health. Tier/loop scaling remains.

Validation
Editor.txt: 570 seeded wave compositions; all eight route branches; exact item/selection/wave sequence; two-second charge; ten unique miniboss types and new native model renders.
Runtime.txt combines passed batched and targeted checks: opening item; labels hidden in flight; full-width two-second skip including boss items; timed escape versus kill and both bonus-choice gates; every district's mobile firing miniboss; real volcano fire; actual HUD countdown; smooth 60-degree reversals; sixteen crab arm meshes; submarine triple eruptions and six-lane charge (peak 102 bullets); marine life; orange carrier exception; no item-based health inflation.
Final.txt records the two adjusted difficulty samples and final offer layout. Native models, scenery, moving-wave snapshots, articulated crab, submarine firing and final item UI were reviewed. No C# or shader compiler diagnostics remained. The isolated Unity editor's existing SearchDatabase startup exception is outside gameplay and was excluded from game-error collection.

Validation was batched; follow-ups covered the specific failed/outstanding cases rather than repeating the full run. No live player run was controlled. This is a reviewed balance pass, not exhaustive testing of every possible loadout combination. Existing Unity serialization/settings changes were preserved in a separate commit.
