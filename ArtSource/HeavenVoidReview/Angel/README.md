# Heaven's eye-covered angel

Miguel requested a biblical angel with rings of eyes rotating around its core. The former winged spacecraft is replaced with a pearl-white central eye, a radiant corona and three interlocking golden wheels carrying another 36 eyes.

The wheels spin independently in opposing directions and slowly precess. Each eye has a modeled lid, white surface, blue iris, dark pupil and highlight. The giant lasers bind to a selected living eye for their full charge and release; the outer eyes deliver the arrow phrases. The eyes and core share the existing boss health, while the empty spaces between them remain open. Phase count, health tuning, attack scores and approved laser sounds remain unchanged.

[Model views](Models.jpg) show four rotation poses from the front and four from an angle. [Gameplay animation](Angel.gif) samples the native Unity review at five frames per second. The original screenshots remain in `Model-*.png`; full gameplay captures are retained locally under the ignored `../Frames/Heaven-Angel-*` directories.

`VerifyHeavenVoid.RunAngel` performs a targeted review in the isolated checkout. `Runtime.txt` records the three-phase checks for rotating wheels, modeled-eye laser attachment, moving damage contacts, engine removal and the existing boss finale. `Tools/render_heaven_angel_review.py` composes the gallery and animation from these actual captures. The Windows build remains at `Build/HeavenVoid/Shootemup.exe`.
