Wind-up Cannon, Popcorn, Icicle, and item feedback review

58 isolated Unity play-mode checks passed. Main project run was not controlled.
Models inspected from two angles. Reviewed actual gameplay frames for charge/release,
frost buildup/shatter, Popcorn, Capacitor, Overdrive, glasses, energy and static cues,
plus combined effects against moving Plains enemies.

Wind-up Cannon: hold either assigned fire input, release to fire. Tiers at 0/.45/1.2s,
2/7/16 damage multipliers, 0/2/5 additional pierces. Fire speed changes charge rate.
Magnifying Glass trades charge rate for power. Double Trouble keeps charging while
any coupled input remains held. Pause stops charge; Bullet Time allows charge/fire
while the resulting rounds remain frozen. Decisions cancel without releasing.

Popcorn: every fifth primary hit creates three short-range pellets, each dealing
35% of hit damage. Burning targets trigger immediately; .12s proc gate. Fragments
cannot recursively trigger more Popcorn or Icicle. Companion hits don't build it.

Icicle: three primary hits build frost, with .12s buildup gate. Ice lasts 1.2s;
next hit shatters for 50% bonus hit damage plus five fragments at 25% damage each.
Ordinary enemies briefly hold position; bosses retain movement. Frost has a .6s
rebuild rest after shattering. Ice and fragments freeze during Bullet Time.

Feedback: Capacitor ready chime, muzzle flash and charged sprite wake; Overdrive
activation/expiry cues and equipment rim; static charge dots and discharge cue;
subtle glasses boundary/entry flecks; energy meter pulse while core regenerates.
Distinct roll, fan, projection, healing and time-freeze sounds.

Audio: 15 sample-based Frontlines cues. Existing Sound Lab choices unchanged.
Audio/manifest.json records source cuts and hashes. TechnicalChecks.txt verifies
mono PCM, sample rate, peak headroom and faded endpoints. Subjective listening and
mix approval are not claimed. ItemCues.wav is an ordered audition reel.
