Heaven scenery, 6 November 2026

Cloud islands now have deep, irregular overlapping billows and smaller wind-torn
curls instead of capped stone chunks. Warm upper lighting, blue undersides and
restrained contact shading connect the bank to its architecture.

Six separate landmarks replace the closely related arch variants: a crowned
processional gate, breached arch, roofless cloister, gilded spire shrine, winged
war memorial and broken arcade. Terraces have paving, inset stairs, column bases
and capitals. Spear-point railings flank the stairs. Broken lances and masonry
preserve the war-in-Heaven identity. The two pennants move in the material shader.

Twelve near and six distant islands use seeded placements, scale, tilt and scroll
speeds. The centre remains open. The cloud ocean uses three gently drifting
relief layers, mip-filtered and uncompressed to remove noisy normal lighting and
block artifacts. Heaven's enemy materials and projectile artwork are unchanged.

Review: offscreen native Unity renders at 405x720, four scroll positions and all
six landmark closeups. The first review exposed excessive grain in the distant
cloud layer; only that visual was revised before the final capture. The final
images and moving sequence were inspected for shape, depth and central space.
No visible gameplay session or desktop controls were used.

Budget: 37 scenery renderers in the entire looping scene (including offscreen
islands), 118106 total triangles, zero lights and zero colliders. Meshes are
created once and shared; scrolling updates only the 18 roots and three material
clocks. No runtime noise baking, mesh uploads or per-prop Update components.
