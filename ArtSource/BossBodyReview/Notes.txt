One body collider per boss - 6 November 2026

Every active boss now owns one root Collider2D and one shared damage target.
Heaven's 37 eye/core colliders are replaced with one continuous body outline.
Desert's reactors, Hell's head and Space's event horizon no longer own separate
health or damage components. The whole worm, crab pincers and accretion disk can
be shot. Each of City's two independent ships retains its own collider and health.

Existing authored outlines and the submarine capsule are retained. Other hulls
use cached mesh outlines, projected with their moving anatomy into one polygon.
Exhaust effects and hidden meshes are excluded. Void preloads every form's outline.
The worm uses one ribbon following its articulated sections; its reactors retire
visually and stop firing with shared health loss. Total starting health is unchanged.
Shared body hit flashes do not move the collider. Homing measures boss proximity
from the damage body, allowing selection beside a wing, pincer or worm section.

Review: 378 passing assertions, all 16 active boss types, 50 phase/body cases,
and the 11 district minibosses. Native gameplay used Normal; phase meter and
invulnerability/targeting regressions separately covered Easy, Normal and Hard.
Verified one collider/one polygon path, one damage owner, native projectile hits
applied once, beam and area contact, nearby-body targeting, phase protection,
and whole-body death cleanup. City was checked with both ships present.

The batch retained completed cases while correcting specific failures: the review
needed to release its suspended campaign decision after deaths; cached body parts
needed a destroyed-mesh guard; the worm's material block needed Start initialization;
and the submarine probe needed to strike before another frame could submerge it.
Only affected or unfinished cases were resumed. Offscreen visual inspection caught
engine flames extending four hull outlines; those effects were excluded and only
Snow Siege, City Duo, Thunder Lance and Candy Regent were rechecked.

Heaven stress: 3600 pooled rounds, 600 complete editor frames including physics.
CPU p95 10.714 ms, maximum 22.643 ms; native physics p95 0.933 ms. No additional
projectile objects were created. Rendering was excluded from this timing sample;
this is not a full rendered gameplay frame-rate measurement.
